TPAP devices

These devices answered discovery, but their firmware uses TPAP encryption, which the installed python-kasa cannot control locally. This is a firmware/protocol limitation, not a missing device or a scan timeout. python-kasa 0.11.0.1 and 0.12.0 exhibit the same limitation.

Changing credentials or repeating the scan will not add TPAP support. KasaBuena shows these hosts for visibility; they are not controllable device cards.
